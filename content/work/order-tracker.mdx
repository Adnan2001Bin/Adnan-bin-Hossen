Order Tracker is a comprehensive supply chain management information system built for the garment industry. It brings order management, bill of materials tracking, vendor coordination, and AI-powered insights into one unified platform.

## The Problem

Garment supply chains involve complex coordination between buyers, vendors, and production teams. Tracking orders, materials, and vendor performance across disconnected tools leads to delays, miscommunication, and missed deadlines.

## Approach

The application was built with React and TypeScript, featuring dedicated vendor portals and management dashboards. AI integration helps surface patterns and insights from order and production data, giving teams actionable information rather than raw numbers.

## What I Worked On

- Built order management workflows with real-time status tracking across the supply chain.
- Implemented BOM (bill of materials) tracking to connect orders with material requirements.
- Developed vendor management portals for external partners to update and view order progress.
- Integrated AI-powered insights to highlight trends, risks, and optimization opportunities.

## Outcome

Order Tracker gives garment industry teams a single source of truth for supply chain operations, reducing coordination overhead and improving visibility from order placement to delivery.

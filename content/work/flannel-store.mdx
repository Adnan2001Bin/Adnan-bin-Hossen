Flannel Store is a premium luxury fashion e-commerce platform designed to showcase high-end designer brands with an elegant, immersive shopping experience. The storefront balances visual appeal with smooth, intuitive navigation.

## The Problem

Luxury fashion e-commerce demands a different standard than typical online stores. Product presentation, brand storytelling, and a frictionless checkout experience are essential to converting discerning customers.

## Approach

The platform was built with React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, emphasizing clean typography, generous whitespace, and high-quality product imagery. The shopping flow was designed to feel premium at every step — from browsing collections to completing a purchase.

## What I Worked On

- Built an elegant product catalog with category filtering and detailed product pages.
- Designed responsive layouts optimized for both desktop browsing and mobile shopping.
- Implemented a smooth checkout flow with cart management and order confirmation.
- Ensured performance and accessibility across the full shopping experience.

## Outcome

Flannel Store delivers a polished e-commerce experience that reflects the premium positioning of the brands it showcases, combining visual elegance with reliable functionality.
